Factory Audit & Process Review

Qualifying a new OEM partner to SumUp standards.

I completed a full remote factory audit for the new OEM that we worked with on this project. This involved a comprehensive review of their existing processes, capabilities, and quality systems to assess their readiness to manufacture SumUp products. Following the audit I led a full process review to bring the new partner up to the company's manufacturing standards, identifying gaps and implementing corrective actions across the production workflow.

Fixture Design & Yield Improvement

Achieving close to 99% first-pass yield.

I implemented new fixtures and testing stations that helped increase the first-pass yield to close to 99%. By designing purpose-built test equipment and refining the test process, we were able to catch defects earlier in the production flow and reduce the number of units requiring rework, significantly improving overall line efficiency.

Warehouse Upgrade Process

A critical upgrade step that kept the product on track for launch.

I designed and implemented a critical upgrade process that was performed in our warehouse to bring devices to the required specification before they were shipped to customers. This process was essential to ensuring the product was able to hit its launch date, and required careful coordination between the manufacturing, logistics, and engineering teams.
