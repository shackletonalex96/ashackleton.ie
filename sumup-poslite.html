POS Lite - Low Cost Android Based POS System

2025

Stood up a full new manufacturing line within a 3-month window, doubling productivity versus the original line and avoiding a stock-out during unprecedented demand. Delivered the project over $1 million under budget.

Main Roles

Manufacturing Engineer

The POS Lite had originally ceased production due to slow sales and a large existing stock. When unprecedented demand in 2025 rapidly depleted that stock, I was part of the team that was tasked with starting up an entirely new manufacturing line at a new factory within a three-month window.

What I did

  • Manufacturing Line Design.
  • Factory Transfer & Equipment Salvage.
  • Production Ramp-Up.
  • Process Optimisation.

Factory Transfer

Moving production to a new OEM partner under a tight deadline.

The original OEM was not interested in continuing production, so we had to transfer the full line to our main manufacturing partner's factory. We had a three-month window to design a new line, move all reusable equipment from the original factory, prepare materials, and ramp up production for a limited four-month run before a stock-out would have impacted sales.

Line Design & Production Ramp-Up

Designing from scratch and doubling productivity.

I designed the new manufacturing line from scratch, salvaging what I could from the original factory and specifying new equipment where needed. Through careful process design I was able to double the productivity compared to the original line. These efforts, combined with the work of the wider supply chain team, allowed us to complete the project within the three-month timeframe and come in over $1 million under our expected budget.
