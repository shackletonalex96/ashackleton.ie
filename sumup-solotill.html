Fixture & Tester Design

Consolidating complex assembly tasks through thoughtful fixture design.

I designed, prototyped, and commissioned all mechanical fixtures and testers for this product. Through careful fixture design I was able to consolidate several risky and complicated assembly tasks into a single station, reducing the number of handling steps and the potential for operator error. This consolidation was key to enabling the line to scale efficiently through all NPI builds without requiring significant rework or redesign at each stage.

NPI Technical Lead

Overseeing the product from first build through to mass production.

I served as the technical lead on all NPI builds of the Solo Till Printer, taking ownership of the manufacturing process from the earliest prototype builds through to full mass production. At each build stage I identified issues, implemented improvements, and ensured that the line was ready to scale. I oversaw the continuous improvement of the manufacturing line throughout, ensuring that lessons learned at each stage were carried forward into the next.
