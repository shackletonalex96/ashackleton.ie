Connector Housing Design

Housing and alignment clip design, tooling & prototyping.

Across multiple projects I was responsible for the full mechanical design of connector housings, from initial concept through to production tooling. This included designing key features such as plug latches, anchor pin attachment points, and alignment clips, all validated through rigorous FEA simulation and physical testing.

I managed the transition from soft-tooled injection moulded prototypes to hard tooling, working closely with external plastics manufacturers to ensure mouldability. Once first articles were available, I generated comprehensive measurement reports, scrutinising each dimension and tolerance to finalise the production moulds. Material selection was also my responsibility, ensuring compliance with application-specific regulations and long-term supply chain viability.

Several projects involved substantial late-stage design changes driven by evolving client requirements. By rapidly prototyping and evaluating new concepts in-house, I was able to accommodate these changes with minimal impact on overall project timelines.

EMI Shield Design

Shield design, rapid prototyping & tooling.

I designed EMI shields from prototype through to production. Initial prototype shields had quality issues that I was able to resolve in-house to ensure first articles were delivered on time. For the production version, I applied lessons learned from prototyping to create a far more robust design, then worked with external manufacturers to ensure the parts could be stamped to our specification and within budget.

FOT Leadframe Design

Leadframe design, component placement, heat transfer analysis & tooling.

I designed leadframes for both Rx and Tx FOTs across multiple connector variants, all manufactured using photochemical etching. I worked alongside the electronic engineering team to develop the internal layout and circuitry of the transceivers, and designed the associated cutting and forming tools for implementation into the company's manufacturing plant in China.

On one project, existing parts had an overheating problem causing operational failures. Using heat transfer and CFD analysis I was able to optimise the leadframe design and component layout to provide maximum heat dissipation, resolving the issue.

Packaging Design

Component tubes, cardboard packaging & shipping procedures.

I designed packaging for individual components, sub-assemblies, and fully assembled connectors across all projects. Each required bespoke plastic packaging tubes to protect fragile leads during transit and handling. All cardboard packaging was also designed along with full packaging and shipping procedures and labelling specifications.

Assembly & Test Fixtures

Assembly fixtures, test fixtures & manufacturing process reviews.

I designed all tooling used in the assembly process across the connector product range. This included multi-stage hand press fixtures for anchor pin and retaining clip assembly, designed to apply sufficient force while preventing damage to housings and electronic components.

I also reviewed and improved manufacturing processes, including the potting process at our factory where I identified improvements and made design changes to help epoxy flow more efficiently. Test fixtures were designed and adapted to allow assembled PCBs and connectors to be verified, enabling effective quality checks for damage or wire bond breaks from soldering and handling.
