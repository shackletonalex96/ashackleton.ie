Manufacturing Line Scale-Up & Process Optimisation

Doubling capacity and driving sustained efficiency improvements.

I was a key member of the team that doubled the capacity of the Solo manufacturing line. Through detailed process analysis I identified opportunities to streamline operations, ultimately achieving a 35% reduction in the value-added time per unit alongside the capacity increase. I later led efforts to validate and introduce new mechanical parts that enabled a complete revision of the line layout, delivering a further 18% reduction in value-added time. This project had been parked for years before we picked it back up. The total effect of these changes amounted to over €100k in savings.

In-House Testing Station

Full mechatronic design of a replica test rig: mechanical, electronic, and embedded software.

I designed and manufactured a replica testing station for the Solo from scratch, completing the full mechanical, electronic, and embedded software design. The tester incorporated button testing, card insertion testing, touchscreen validation, NFC testing, and buzzer detection, replicating the full scope of the production-line test sequence. This rig gave the manufacturing test engineers the ability to trial and validate any software changes in-house before pushing them to the live production line, significantly reducing the risk associated with test software updates. My original rig was used to validate all software changes and prove the concept. I then worked alongside our manufacturing test equipment supplier to create a more industrialised version based on my design for deployment on the production line.
