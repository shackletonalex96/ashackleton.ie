Manufacturing Line Design

Full design of a flexible, multi-product manufacturing line.

I was responsible for the full design of a multi-product manufacturing line for the MFD and CFD. The line was designed to be flexible, capable of speeding up or slowing down without significant impact on overall productivity, so that it could handle the initial spike in production at launch and still run efficiently as product demand wound down. I led automation efforts on the line that increased productivity by 45%, and I scoped, audited, and onboarded multiple new specialist suppliers for key assembly tasks.

DFA Reviews

Introducing structured design-for-assembly reviews into the company.

I introduced a structured approach to performing DFA reviews within SumUp, working closely with the mechanical design team to integrate this new practice into the company-wide design process. Through these reviews I caught and resolved multiple assembly and testing issues before they became problems on the production line, saving significant time and cost during the manufacturing ramp-up.

In-Office Functional Tester

Solving a problem our suppliers said was impossible.

I built a functional test rig within an existing in-office test station to perform touchscreen testing, USB port testing (power and data), and button testing on the device. The button test was particularly challenging due to the button's location, on the back side of the screen, within a 7mm gap between the screen and the body. Several of our suppliers stated that automating this test was impossible. I was able to design, prototype, and validate a solution, which subsequently allowed us to fully automate the button test on the production line.

PCBA Test Fixture

A low-cost fixture that saved €40k on a single build.

I designed, manufactured, and validated a general-purpose PCBA test fixture that could be used for early product builds as well as R&D activity in the office for future projects. This fixture ended up saving €40k on one early build alone. We were using prototype boards but had no way of flashing them at scale. Several of my low-cost fixtures (under €400 each) were sent to China for this build, allowing it to proceed as planned and avoiding the cost of commissioning a bespoke industrial tester for these units.

Product Fault Investigations

Root cause analysis on field returns during product launch.

During the product launch I conducted multiple product fault investigations on devices returned from the field. I identified one critical mechanical flaw and worked closely with the design team to quickly rectify it. I also identified several process-related issues that were root-caused back to quality lapses at the factory. I worked with the OEM management team to introduce better training and safeguards to reduce the risk of recurrence.
