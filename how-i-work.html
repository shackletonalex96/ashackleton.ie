How I Work

My experience spans the full product development cycle, from early user research and concept design through to mass production. I've built prototyping capabilities from scratch, owned mechanical designs end-to-end, and scaled products from first prototype to hundreds of thousands of units. This breadth means I can contribute at any stage of a project and, more importantly, make design decisions with a clear understanding of their downstream impact on manufacturing, cost, and quality.

01 — Discover

User research, problem framing, stakeholder mapping.

Understanding the real problem before designing a solution. Building empathy maps, personas, and identifying genuine points of friction for users.

Bélú Solutions - Real Time Food Waste Analytics   Hydra Diagnostics - Reverse Osmosis Blood Testing Unit

02 — Design

Concept generation, industrial & mechanical design, CAD.

Translating user needs into physical concepts. Mechanical design, industrial design, and detailed CAD development of housings, mechanisms, and assemblies.

Fibre Optic Connectors   Sensi - Reverse Vending Machine

03 — Prototype

Rapid iteration, in-house prototyping capability.

Turning concepts into testable hardware quickly. I've built in-house prototyping capability from scratch at three organisations, cutting design cycle times dramatically.

In-House Prototyping

04 — Validate

Testing, DFM/DFA, FEA, characterisation.

Proving designs work and can be built. Structured DFA/DFM reviews, FEA and CFD simulation, and the design of bespoke test fixtures and rigs.

Electronics Testing   MFD & CFD - Android Based POS and Payment System

05 — Productionise

Tooling, line design, DFM, scale-up.

Taking products to mass production reliably and efficiently. Manufacturing line design, tooling, automation, and scaling from first build to hundreds of thousands of units.

Solo - Standalone Handheld Payment Device   POS Lite - Low Cost Android Based POS System
