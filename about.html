About Me

Over 6 years of experience in product development, prototyping, and manufacturing engineering. I've worked in fields like fintech hardware, optoelectronics, water infrastructure, and early-stage startups.

I cover the entire product development cycle, from concept design to mass production. I've developed prototyping capabilities from the ground up, managed mechanical designs, and scaled products from the first prototype to hundreds of thousands of units. This experience allows me to contribute at any project stage. I make informed design decisions that consider their effects on manufacturing, cost, and quality.
