Lilongwe Water Board - Water Treatment Works III

2021

Designed MEICA systems for a 50,000m³/day water treatment plant, developing a site-wide hydraulic profile and wastewater management system. Analysed 10 years of water quality data to select treatment processes, and sized site power and backup generation.

Main Roles

MEICA Design

Responsible for all mechanical and electrical aspects of the design relating to the water treatment process.

What I did

  • Hydraulic analysis.
  • Pumps/Instrumentation.
  • Process analysis.
  • Power analysis.

Visit Project Site

Hydraulic Analysis

Pipe sizing, site-wide hydraulic profiles, waste water management system.

I sized all pipework on site and liaised with senior CAD technicians on the pipe layout. I developed a comprehensive hydraulic profile of the treatment to check the headlosses and gravity fed flows throughout the entire treatment works. I designed a waste management system for the site to handle the sludge from the pre-sedimentation and sedimentation processes, emergency overflows and wastewater from the filter backwashing.

Pumps/Instrumentation

Sizing, control philosophy.

I prepared specifications for all the pump needs for the treatment works and consulted with local suppliers to choose the appropriate model. I worked alongside the senior MEICA engineer to select the sensors to be used on site and develop a site wide control philosophy.

Process Analysis

Water quality data processing, process reviews, chemical CAPEX/OPEX comparison study.

I analysed and processed 10 years of daily water quality readings from the existing plants. Using this data appropriate water treatment processes were selected. I then performed detailed cost benefit analysis of several treatment processes in consultation with local chemical suppliers.

Power Analysis

Site power, backup generators, renewable energy audit.

I sized and selected the transformer and backup power generator to be installed onsite. I then performed solar power potential study for the site and prepared a report to be sent to the client for consideration.

Documentation

Preparation of tender documents and detailed technical specifications.

I prepared documentation for the project's inception, preliminary design and detailed design stages.
