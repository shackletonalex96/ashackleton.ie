Pricing Structure Overhaul

From hourly rates to transactional pricing: over 100 individual job rates.

I led a complete overhaul of the pricing structure for SumUp's third-party warehouse operations. The existing model was based on a simple hourly rate, which provided no visibility into the actual cost of individual operations and no incentive for efficiency. I transitioned the entire operation to a transactional, per-unit pricing model. Every single warehouse operation had to be timed, broken down into individual tasks, and a unit price agreed upon with the 3PL partner. Over 100 individual job rates were created as part of this process. Implementation began in Q4 2025 and delivered a 10% reduction in overall warehousing costs in 2025 as a whole. Comparing month-to-month figures between 2025 and 2026, the monthly invoice reduced by 35-50% despite a marked increase in both inbound and outbound goods volumes.

Automation & Productivity Improvement

A 70% increase in picking and outbound productivity, plus €200k in annual savings.

I conducted an in-depth analysis of all warehouse processes and identified picking and outbound operations as accounting for a disproportionate share of our costs. I researched and onboarded several reputable automation suppliers, evaluated their solutions against our requirements, and led the implementation of automation across these critical processes. The result was a 70% increase in productivity and approximately €200k in annual savings. I also pushed our 3PL partner to engage with and invest in these automation activities, ensuring alignment and long-term sustainability of the improvements.
