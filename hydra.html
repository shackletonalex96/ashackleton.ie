Hydra Diagnostics - Reverse Osmosis Blood Testing Unit

2020

Built a working testing rig replicating a proprietary blood-hydration measurement method, engineering a precision-milled transfer unit to strict pressure and flow tolerances. Fully automated the test process with microcontroller-driven sensors and a touchscreen UI.

About

Hydra Diagnostics enlisted the services of TCD in the development of several working prototypes for their product, through EI's Innovation Voucher programme. They had developed a proprietary method for measuring a patient's hydration level from a blood sample and it was our job to create a testing rig to replicate their results quickly and accurately.

Design Development

Phase 1:

The first phase of the project was focused on the mechanical design of the testing rig. The transfer unit was the most critical part of this, it had to be rigid, watertight and operate under a specific pressure and flow rates in order for the proposed testing method to work. The unit had to be precisely milled out of a solid block of polycarbonate and the design was refined over several design iterations. The testing rig was then assembled and the proposed methods were validated. Water mixed with pectin and dye was used as stand-in for blood.

Phase 2:

In phase 1 tests had to be fully supervised and all data recorded manually, by using a microcontroller (Arduino) it was possible to completely automate the process. Designing such a system became the primary focus of this second stage of development. The pumps in the rig were linked to and controlled by the main sensors. Data was captured and logged to an SD card in an easily readable format. The whole testing rig was controlled by a touchscreen UI which not only allowed the user to choose when the test began but also gave feedback on the progress of the test and also on the status of the rig in general (is there room on the SD card, has a sample been loaded, etc.) The user could also calibrate the sensors using this interface.
