2D Drawing Standards

Creating the company's first standardised drawing SOPs.

I was responsible for generating the first set of SOPs for 2D drawings within SumUp. This involved standardising the format, tolerancing approach, and annotation conventions used across all mechanical drawings produced by the design team. These SOPs are now used company-wide and have significantly improved the quality and consistency of the documentation that accompanies mechanical designs through to manufacturing.

FEA Introduction

Pioneering the use of finite element analysis within the company.

I pioneered the use of FEA within SumUp, identifying a gap in the company's design validation process and making the case for its adoption. I sourced and scoped a list of potential software suppliers, led all trial runs and benchmarking activities, and subsequently led the introduction of FEA into the standard design cycle. This has given the engineering team the ability to validate designs and identify potential failure modes earlier in the development process, allowing them to refine their designs further and have more confidence before tools are cut.
